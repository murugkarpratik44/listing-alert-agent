# Ireland rentals – viewing and enquiry checklist

Checked 2026-10-17. General information, not legal advice. Each rule links to its source; check the source before relying on it.

## Money rules

| Rule | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Deposit limit | A deposit cannot be more than one month's rent. | [RTB FAQ, Residential Tenancies (No. 2) Act 2021](https://www.rtb.ie/images/uploads/general/RTA22021_FAQs_July_2021_Update_(2).pdf) |
| Advance rent limit | Advance rent cannot be more than one month's rent, so no more than two months' rent in total to secure a tenancy (student-specific accommodation can differ). Applies to tenancies created from 9 August 2021. | RTB FAQ above; [UCC summary](https://www.ucc.ie/en/accommodation/newsevents/changes-to-the-residential-tenancies-act.html) |
| Registration | The landlord must register a new tenancy with the RTB within one month of the start date, and again each year on the anniversary. | [RTB – When to register](https://rtb.ie/register-tenancies/register-a-tenancy/when-to-register/) |
| BER in the advert | An advert for a home to let must show its BER (or "BER Exempt"). | [SEAI BER Combined Advertising Guidelines (S.I. 243 of 2012, Article 12)](https://www.seai.ie/sites/default/files/2026-07/BER-Combined-Advertising-Guidelines.pdf) |

## Scam warning signs (Garda advice)

From An Garda Síochána advice reported by [KildareNow, 26 August 2026](https://www.kildarenow.com/news/kildare-gardai-issue-rental-advice-to-all-new-third-level-students-8942612):

- If the price seems far too good to be true, it probably is.
- Always view the property in person before agreeing to rent it.
- Never transfer money to "secure" a property before viewing it.
- Do not pay a deposit until you have a genuine written lease or rental agreement.
- Avoid paying deposits in cash.
- Receive the keys in person and check they open the property.
- Watch for fake contracts, fake keys and convincing online adverts.

## Questions to ask

- Is the rent inclusive of any bills? Which ones?
- What is the BER, and how is the home heated (gas, electric, heat pump)?
- When is the tenancy available, and for how long (fixed term or Part 4)?
- Who are the other occupants, if any? Is parking included?
- Is the tenancy registered with the RTB, or will it be within one month?
- What deposit and advance rent are required (within the limits above)? How will the deposit be paid?
- What furniture and appliances are included? Any known repairs outstanding?

## Documents to have ready

Landlords and agents commonly ask for: photo ID, a reference from a previous landlord, an employer reference or recent payslips, and recent bank statements. Share copies only after viewing and only with a verified landlord or agent; blank out account numbers you do not need to show.
